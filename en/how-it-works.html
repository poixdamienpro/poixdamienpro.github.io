How it works

From technical requirement to manufacturer quote in three steps, free and with no account required.

Buy-ineer is built for engineers and industrial buyers looking for a specific component: a 48 V LiFePO4 battery, a 32 A PDU, a V2G on-board charger, a certified servo valve. Instead of opening ten PDF datasheets and calling three sales reps, you filter, compare and get in touch from a single place.

// STEP 1

Explore the directory and the catalog

The directory lists manufacturers and equipment makers with their areas of expertise, certifications and verified contact details. The catalog gathers their products into 8 component families (batteries, PDUs, OBCs, valves & actuators, converters, cabling, sensors, motors). Every profile is reviewed before publication.

// STEP 2

Compare specs side by side

Product profiles show the real specifications: chemistry and capacity for a battery, amperage and number of outputs for a PDU, input voltage and standards for a controller. Filter by category, compare several references, and lean on our "how to choose" guides for each component family to weigh the criteria that matter.

// STEP 3

Request a quote in one click

Found the right product? Click "Request a quote", describe your requirements (quantity, technical constraints, lead time) and your request is relayed to the selected manufacturer, and to them alone. No account to create, no mailing list to join, no commission on your order. The full details are on the quote requests & RFQs page.

// AND FOR MANUFACTURERS?

A pay-per-result model on the supplier side

Listing a company and its products is free. A supplier only pays when they accept a lead, in other words a qualified quote request they choose to handle. Everything is explained on the become a supplier page and the pricing grid.
