Frequently asked questions

How Buy-inner works, on the buyer side and the supplier side.

// FOR BUYERS

Is Buy-inner really free for buyers?

Yes, and forever. Browsing the manufacturer directory, comparing product specifications and sending a quote request is 100% free, with no account required.

Do I need to create an account to request a quote?

No. You provide your name, your work email, your company and your requirements (quantity, technical constraints, lead time). The request is relayed to the selected manufacturer, who decides whether to contact you back directly.

What happens to my quote request once it is sent?

It is relayed to the manufacturer you selected, and to them alone. You are never added to a mailing list and your contact details are never resold. The manufacturer then decides whether or not to contact you back.

// FOR SUPPLIERS

How much does it cost for a supplier to get listed?

Nothing. The company profile and product profiles are free, no matter what. A supplier only pays when they accept a lead: around €80 per accepted quote request. No mandatory subscription, no hidden fees. Details are on the pricing page.

Can a supplier decline a lead?

Yes, at any time. The supplier reviews each request (requirements, volume, contact details) before deciding. Declining costs nothing: only a lead that is explicitly accepted is billed.

How are company and product profiles vetted?

Every listing is reviewed before publication: consistency of the specifications, legitimacy of the company, quality of the profile. Buy-inner is not a mass self-populated directory. To get listed, head to the become a supplier page.

// THE PLATFORM

What types of industrial components are listed?

Eight families: batteries and energy storage, power distribution units (PDU), on-board chargers (OBC), valves and actuators, converters and inverters, cables and connectors, sensors and instrumentation, motors and drives, serving electric mobility, rail, aerospace, space, energy and industry.

Who is behind Buy-inner?

Buy-inner is an independent project, with no equity ties to the manufacturers listed. More details on the about us page, or by email: contact@buy-inner.com.
