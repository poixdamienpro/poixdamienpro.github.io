On-board chargers (OBC)

On-board AC→DC chargers for electric vehicles, from single-phase to bidirectional V2G.

The OBC (On-Board Charger) converts AC power from the grid into DC to charge the traction battery. Recent models are bidirectional (V2G / V2L), letting the vehicle feed energy back. Power, efficiency and single/three-phase compatibility are the deciding criteria.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • PowerFrom 3.3 kW to 22 kW and above.
  • Input supplySingle-phase or three-phase.
  • Battery voltage range400 V / 800 V compatibility.
  • EfficiencyTypically > 94%.
  • BidirectionalV2G (grid), V2L (external loads).
  • CoolingAir or liquid.
  • CommunicationCAN bus, BMS supervision.
  • StandardsCISPR 25, ISO 6469, ISO 26262.
