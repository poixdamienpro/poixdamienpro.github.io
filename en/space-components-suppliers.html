// WHAT MATTERS IN THIS INDUSTRY

Space-specific selection criteria

  • Flight heritageNumber of units in orbit and reference missions — the No. 1 criterion for de-risking a design.
  • QualificationECSS standards, vibration/shock testing, thermal vacuum, radiation tolerance (TID, SEE).
  • Mission classCommercial CubeSat/LEO (qualified COTS) vs GEO/institutional (full space grade) — cost varies by a factor of 10.
  • Mass & volumeEvery gram counts: battery energy density, equipment compactness.
  • InterfacesPower buses (28 V, 50 V, 100 V), CAN/SpaceWire protocols, CubeSat standards (PC/104).
  • Lead times & ITAROften long lead times; export restrictions depending on component origin.
