SERVICES

Four ways to move faster

  • Directory and catalogueManufacturers, system integrators and industrial service providers, with comparable technical sheets, datasheets, the supplier map and side-by-side product comparison. Available with a free account.
  • RFQ / RFI / RFP tendersAre you a system integrator who needs to source a component or a service from several suppliers? File one request: it is visible to the listed suppliers of the relevant category, protected by a confidentiality agreement, and the answers arrive comparable in your dashboard. Learn more
  • Equipment transformation supportQualify or transform existing equipment to adapt it to another domain: frame the requirements, measure the gaps, define the test plan and identify the service providers. Offered on quote, with no commitment. Learn more
