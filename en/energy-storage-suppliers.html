// WHAT MATTERS IN THIS INDUSTRY

Energy-specific selection criteria

  • Storage certificationsIEC 62619, UL 1973/9540A (ESS fire safety), UN38.3 for transport.
  • Lifetime & cyclingNumber of cycles at a given depth of discharge, the parameter that drives the cost per stored kWh.
  • Grid connectionGrid-code compliance, power quality, anti-islanding protection.
  • SupervisionMonitoring (Modbus, CAN, IP), SCADA/EMS integration, remote maintenance.
  • EnvironmentIP rating, temperature range, indoor/outdoor or containerized installation.
  • Warranty & supportMulti-year capacity warranty, maintenance network, spare parts availability.
