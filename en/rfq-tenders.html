RFQ / RFI / RFP tenders

Reach every qualified supplier in your sector from one dossier, with a built-in confidentiality agreement.

Are you a system integrator sourcing a sub-assembly, a critical component or a service from several suppliers at once? Instead of emailing the same spec sheet to a dozen contacts, post an RFQ, RFI or RFP dossier on Buy-inner — it becomes visible to every listed supplier in the relevant category, who respond directly from the platform.

// STEP 1

Post your dossier

Title, type (RFQ for a quote request, RFI for an information request, RFP for a proposal request), category, description of your requirement, response deadline and, if needed, a spec sheet attachment.

// STEP 2

Reviewed before publication

Like every company and product profile on Buy-inner, your dossier is reviewed before it goes live — nothing gets published without validation.

// STEP 3

Suppliers review and respond

Connected suppliers see your dossier once it's published, but its full content (complete description, spec sheet) is only revealed after they accept a confidentiality agreement. They then respond directly from their account, with a message and an indicative price.

// STEP 4

Compare and choose

Every response lands in your dashboard. Accept the one that fits, decline the others, or close the dossier once you've made your choice — everything stays tracked, nothing gets lost in an inbox.

// CONFIDENTIALITY

A confidentiality agreement, not an open inbox

A dossier's full content (complete description, spec sheet) is never visible until a supplier has accepted a confidentiality agreement. By accepting it, they commit to :

  • No disclosurenot sharing the dossier's content with third parties not involved in their response.
  • Limited useonly using the information to respond commercially to that specific dossier.
  • No direct contactnot contacting the integrator outside the platform based on this information.

The agreement is signed once per supplier account, before accessing the very first dossier — no need to re-accept it for every new dossier. Until it's accepted, no dossier is visible, not even its title.

For a more sensitive requirement, the integrator can additionally require a custom NDA specific to their dossier, instead of relying on the standard agreement alone. In that case, the supplier downloads the document provided by the integrator, signs it outside the platform, then uploads their signed copy. The full content stays hidden until the integrator has personally reviewed and approved that signature — an extra, dossier-by-dossier check, rather than one generic agreement valid everywhere.

// HOW IT DIFFERS

RFQ dossier or quote request?

A quote request targets one specific supplier, from their company or product profile — ideal when you already know who to contact. An RFQ/RFI/RFP dossier instead reaches every qualified supplier in a category at once — ideal for putting several candidates in competition on a structured requirement.
