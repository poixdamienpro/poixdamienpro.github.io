Industrial batteries & energy storage

Cells, packs and energy storage systems (ESS) for mobility, energy and embedded applications.

An industrial battery is more than its capacity : the chemistry (LiFePO4, Li-ion NMC, LTO), the BMS architecture and the cycle life determine the real service life and the cost per kWh. This category covers cells and packs for electric vehicles as well as stationary energy storage systems (ESS) and space-qualified batteries.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • ChemistryLiFePO4, Li-ion NMC, LTO: the density / safety / cycle-life trade-off.
  • Capacity & energyAh per cell, kWh at pack level.
  • Nominal voltageSeries configuration (e.g. 16S ≈ 51.2 V).
  • Cycle lifeNumber of cycles at a given depth of discharge.
  • Temperature rangeOperation and charging, e.g. -20 to +60 °C.
  • BMS & communicationCell protection, balancing, CAN / Modbus bus.
  • Ingress protectionIP67 and above for embedded use.
  • CertificationsUN38.3, IEC 62619, UL 1973.
