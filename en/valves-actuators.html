Valves & actuators

Servo valves, proportional valves and actuators for fluid and motion control.

This category covers the components that regulate a fluid or a motion : high-dynamics servo valves, proportional valves and electric, hydraulic or pneumatic actuators. Response time, rated flow and operating pressure drive the choice for each application.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • TypeServo valve, proportional, on/off.
  • Rated flowFlow at a given differential pressure.
  • Operating pressureMaximum permissible pressure (bar).
  • Response timeValve bandwidth / dynamics.
  • Control signal±10 V, 4-20 mA, PWM, fieldbus.
  • Body & materialSteel, stainless steel, fluid compatibility.
  • ProtectionIP rating, ATEX for hazardous areas.
  • InterfaceMounting, hydraulic connections.
