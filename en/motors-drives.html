Motors & drives

Electric motors, variable frequency drives and gearmotors for industry and traction.

This category covers electric motors (induction, PMSM/BLDC) and their variable frequency drives (VFD). From industrial pumps to traction drivetrains, it is power, torque, speed range and efficiency class (IE) that make the difference.
