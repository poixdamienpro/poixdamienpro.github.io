Converters & inverters

DC-DC, DC-AC and AC-DC power conversion for energy, traction and industry.

Power converters adapt the voltage and current waveform between a source and a load : DC-DC converters, DC-AC inverters and AC-DC rectifiers. Efficiency, galvanic isolation and electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) sit at the heart of the selection process.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • TopologyDC-DC, DC-AC (inverter), AC-DC.
  • PowerFrom kW to MW depending on the application.
  • Voltage rangesInput and output.
  • EfficiencyOften >96% at nominal load.
  • Galvanic isolationWhether isolated, and to what level.
  • CoolingAir, liquid, conduction.
  • Output qualityTHD, residual ripple.
  • EMC standardsCISPR, IEC 61000.
