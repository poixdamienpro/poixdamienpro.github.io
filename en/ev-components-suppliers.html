Electric vehicle component suppliers

Cells, packs, on-board chargers and power electronics, from cars to buses and trains.

Electrified mobility relies on a complete traction chain : battery cells and packs, on-board charger (OBC), DC/DC converters, high-voltage distribution. Buy-inner lists the cell giants (CATL, Samsung SDI, LG Energy Solution), emerging European players (Verkor) and power electronics specialists (Brusa Elektronik, Delta Electronics, Valeo). These suppliers equip cars, but also buses, trucks and rail (Forsee Power).

// WHAT MATTERS IN THIS INDUSTRY

EV-specific selection criteria

  • Voltage architecture400 V or 800 V: drives the OBC, converters, wiring and charging speed.
  • Cell chemistryLFP (cost, cycle life) vs NMC (density): the range / price / safety trade-off.
  • Charging speedC-rate capability, DC fast charging, V2G/V2L compatibility for the OBC.
  • Functional safetyISO 26262 (ASIL), HV protection, thermal management and runaway prevention.
  • Type approvalsR100 (electric vehicle), UN38.3 (transport), automotive EMC.
  • Volume & sourcingProduction capacity, location (EU local content), multi-year price commitments.
