Equipment transformation support

Qualify or transform existing equipment to adapt it to another domain. On quote.

Equipment designed for one domain cannot always be used as is in another: requirements, environments and the evidence to provide all change. Buy-inner supports you in qualifying or transforming existing equipment so that it meets the expectations of the target domain (space, aerospace and defense, rail, automotive, energy…), relying on the service providers listed on the platform.

WHAT THE SUPPORT COVERS

Qualify, transform, or both

  • Frame the target domainIdentify the requirements, standards and evidence expected in the domain where you want to use the equipment.
  • Measure the gapsCompare the equipment as it exists with the requirements of the target domain, and spot what has to change or be demonstrated.
  • QualifyDefine the relevant test and qualification plan, and identify the laboratories and providers able to carry it out.
  • TransformSpecify the necessary adaptations (components, materials, interfaces, documentation) and the partners to carry them out.
HOW IT WORKS

  • 2. We clarify the needWe get back to you at the address provided to understand the context, the constraints and the timeline.
  • 3. You receive a quoteThe support is offered on quote, tailored to your equipment and your target domain.
  • No commitmentSending a request commits you to nothing: you remain free to accept the quote or not.
