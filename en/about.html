About us

Buy-inner, the industrial components marketplace, built by and for engineers.

Buy-inner is a B2B directory and marketplace dedicated to industrial components : batteries, power distribution units (PDU), on-board chargers (OBC), valves and actuators, converters, cabling, sensors, motors. Our conviction is simple : finding the right industrial component should be as simple as ordering a book online. Today it still takes weeks of PDFs, sales calls and Excel spreadsheets.

// THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Source in minutes, not weeks

In industry, technical specifications are scattered across PDF datasheets, paper catalogs and email threads. Comparing three battery or PDU suppliers takes days. Buy-inner centralizes product listings with their real specs (chemistry, voltage, current rating, certifications…), makes them comparable side by side, and puts the buyer directly in touch with the manufacturer through a one-click quote request.

// OUR PRINCIPLES

What will never change

  • Free for buyersBrowsing the directory, comparing specs and requesting a quote is 100 % free, with no account required.
  • Pay per resultA supplier only pays for a lead they have accepted: no mandatory subscription, no hidden fees. See our pricing.
  • Reviewed listingsEvery company and every product is reviewed before publication. No mass-generated content.
  • No spamA quote request is forwarded to the relevant manufacturer, and to them alone. No mailing-list sign-up.
// TODAY

Where does Buy-inner stand ?

The platform already lists more than 70 manufacturers and equipment makers and more than 250 products across 8 component families, from railway battery packs to aerospace servo valves. The project is developed fully independently : no equity ties to the manufacturers listed, and listings judged on their specs.
