Quote requests & RFQs

Contact the manufacturer of an industrial component in one click, free and with no account required.

On Buy-ineer, every company profile and every product profile has a "Request a quote" button. Describe your requirements (quantity, technical constraints, lead time) and your request is relayed to the selected manufacturer, who decides whether to contact you back directly. It works like a targeted RFQ: no generic form, no purchasing middleman, no commission taken on your order.

// WHAT IT CHANGES

Why go through Buy-ineer?

  • Free & no accountNo sign-up. You leave your name, your work email, your company and your requirements. That's it.
  • A single recipientThe request is relayed to the manufacturer you selected, and to them alone. You are never added to a mailing list.
  • A qualified requestThe manufacturer receives a structured requirement (target product, volume, constraints), so they can reply with a real quote, not a brochure.
  • No commitmentThe manufacturer decides whether or not to contact you back; you remain free to compare several quotes.
// GETTING IT RIGHT

What makes a good quote request

The more precise your request, the faster and more useful the manufacturer's answer. What matters:

  • QuantityPrototype, pre-series or recurring volume: the pricing changes completely.
  • Technical constraintsVoltage, capacity, temperature range, ingress protection, footprint…
  • Required certificationsUN38.3, IEC, UL, ECSS, DO-160… depending on your industry (EV, rail, space, aerospace).
  • Lead timeNeed-by date and project milestones. If time is short, the manufacturer can suggest an off-the-shelf alternative.
// WHERE TO START

Find the right product, then ask

Browse the product catalog or the manufacturer directory, filter by family (batteries, PDUs, OBCs, valves & actuators, sensors…), compare specs, then click "Request a quote" from the profile you're interested in.
