Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28/06/2026

This policy describes what personal data is collected on Buy-inner (www.buy-inner.com), why, and how to exercise your rights. The data controller is Damien Poix, contact: contact@buy-inner.com.

1. Data collected

The site collects personal data only when you provide it voluntarily, in the following cases:

  • Quote request (“Request a quote” form): name, business email, company, description of your need. This information is sent by email to the site manager (via the third-party service Web3Forms) and then passed on to the supplier concerned if they accept the contact.
  • Listing a supplier profile (“List my company” form): information about the company and its products, and the submitter's email.
  • Supplier area (login, listing claim): user email, linked to the company listing concerned.
  • Anonymous audience measurement: for each page visited, the page viewed, the referring page (referrer) and the browser user-agent are recorded for internal statistical purposes. No IP address is stored, and no tracking cookie is set for this measurement.

The site does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. A technical session identifier (sessionStorage, deleted when the browser is closed) is used solely to keep a supplier or administrator account logged in during the browsing session.

2. Purposes of processing

  • Connect a buyer with a listed supplier, at the buyer's explicit request
  • Process requests to list a company and to claim a company listing
  • Measure site audience in an aggregated, anonymous way, to guide its development

3. Legal basis

Processing linked to forms (quote, listing, claim) is based on your explicit consent at the time the form is submitted. Anonymous audience measurement is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used, and does not constitute individual profiling (the data collected does not make it possible to identify a specific visitor).

4. Recipients and processors

Data may be transmitted to the following technical providers, strictly to the extent necessary for the site to operate:

  • Supabase Inc. — hosting of the database and authentication
  • Web3Forms — third-party service used to send quote-request emails
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) — hosting of the static site

No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

5. Retention period

Data linked to a quote request or a listing submission is kept for as long as necessary to process it, then archived or deleted. Anonymous audience measurement data is kept for 13 months at most.

6. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data. You can exercise these rights by writing to contact@buy-inner.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the site or to the regulations. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
