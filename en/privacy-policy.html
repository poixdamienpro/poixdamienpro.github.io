Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04/10/2026

This policy describes what personal data is collected on Buy-inner (www.buy-inner.com), why, and how to exercise your rights. The data controller is Damien Poix, contact: contact@buy-inner.com.

1. Data collected

The site collects personal data in the following cases:

  • Buyer account (“My account” page): name, email, company and password. The password is handled by the Supabase authentication service, which keeps only an encrypted hash of it; the publisher has no access to it. Quote requests sent from an account are linked to that account, which makes it possible to show their history.
  • Quote request (“Request a quote” form): name, business email, company, description of your need, and the supplier and product concerned. This information is saved in our database and sent by email to the site manager (via the third-party service Web3Forms), then passed on to the supplier concerned if they accept the contact.
  • Listing a supplier profile (“List my company” form): information about the company and its products, and the submitter's email.
  • Supplier area (login, listing claim, Premium subscription): user email, linked to the company listing concerned. For a Premium subscription, payment is made on Stripe's secure page: the publisher neither sees nor stores your card number and receives only the subscription status.
  • Company team: a listing's administrator (Premium plan) can invite colleagues. The email address entered is used only to send the invitation and to link the invited account to the company; the administrator can see the email addresses of their team members.
  • Service emails (submission confirmation, listing published, claim approved, team invitation): your email address is passed to our email delivery provider (Resend) to send these messages.
  • Internal audience measurement: for each page visited, the page viewed, the referring page (referrer) and the browser user-agent are recorded for statistical purposes. No IP address is stored in this database.
  • Google Analytics 4 audience measurement: the site loads Google Analytics 4 on all its pages. It is loaded only after you agree, via the banner shown on your first visit. In that case, Google sets cookies (_ga and _ga_*) and processes browsing data (page views, duration, device type, country, traffic source, IP address) to produce statistics. Journey events linked to the free account (a lock being shown, sign-up) are also measured.

2. Cookies and browser storage

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*): audience measurement, set only if you accept them. Their lifetime can be up to 2 years, but your choice is asked again after 13 months (acceptance) or 6 months (refusal). You can change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the page; declining deletes any cookies already set.
  • Local storage (localStorage): the buyer account session (login tokens, identifier) is kept in your browser until you log out, so that you are not asked for your credentials again; the language you chose is also remembered there. The team invitation link received by email is kept there temporarily until you log in.
  • Session storage (sessionStorage): a technical session identifier, deleted when the browser is closed, keeps a supplier or administrator account logged in while you browse.

The site does not use advertising cookies. Audience measurement cookies are never set before you agree; declining does not limit access to the site in any way.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Create and manage your buyer account, and give you access to the detailed information on listings and to the supplier map
  • Connect a buyer with a listed supplier, at the buyer's explicit request
  • Process requests to list a company, to claim a listing and to subscribe to Premium
  • Send the service emails linked to these operations
  • Measure site audience to guide its development (anonymous internal measurement, and Google Analytics with your consent)

4. Legal basis

Account creation, quote requests, listing, claims and subscriptions are based on performing the service you request and, for forms, on your explicit consent when the form is submitted. Anonymous internal audience measurement is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. Google Analytics is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (see section 2).

5. Recipients and processors

Data may be transmitted to the following technical providers, strictly to the extent necessary for the site to operate:

  • Supabase Inc. — database and authentication
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — technical relay of requests between the site and the database (Cloudflare Workers)
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) — hosting of the static site
  • Web3Forms — sending of quote-request emails
  • Resend — sending of service emails
  • Stripe — payment of the Premium subscription
  • Google LLC — Google Analytics 4 and Google Fonts
  • Logo.dev — display of company logos, based on the domain name of the company concerned
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap and unpkg — map tiles and display library for the supplier map

When a page is displayed, some of these providers (fonts, logos, map tiles, libraries) technically receive your browser's IP address. Several are based in the United States; transfers of data outside the European Union rely, as the case may be, on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses. No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

6. Retention period

  • Buyer account: until the account is deleted at your request.
  • Quote requests and listing submissions: as long as necessary to process them, then archived or deleted.
  • Audience measurement: 13 months at most (internal database), 14 months at most (Google Analytics).
  • Billing data: kept for the applicable statutory periods.

7. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data, including deletion of your buyer account. You can exercise these rights by writing to contact@buy-inner.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

8. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the site or to the regulations. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
