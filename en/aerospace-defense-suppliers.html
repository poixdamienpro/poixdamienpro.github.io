Aerospace & defense suppliers

Onboard power, flight controls, electrical distribution and computers for aviation and defense.

In aerospace and defense, a component is selected as much on its certifications as on its performance : DO-160 environmental standards, DO-311A batteries, MIL-STD military standards, AS/EN 9100 quality. Buy-ineer lists world-class equipment makers (Thales, Honeywell Aerospace, Safran Electrical & Power, Moog, Saft) with the real specifications of their products, so you can shortlist without waiting for a sales meeting.

// WHAT MATTERS IN THIS INDUSTRY

Aerospace-specific selection criteria

  • CertificationsDO-160 (environment), DO-311A (batteries), MIL-STD-810/461, EASA/FAA depending on the application.
  • Temperature rangeTypically -55 °C to +70 °C and beyond, with altitude/pressure cycling.
  • Reliability & MTBFDemonstrated failure rate, redundancy, documented failure modes.
  • MassEvery kilogram on board is paid for in fuel or payload.
  • ObsolescenceProgram lifetimes of 30 years and more: availability commitments and end-of-life management.
  • Export & ITARExport controls (ITAR, EAR, dual-use) depending on origin and application.
