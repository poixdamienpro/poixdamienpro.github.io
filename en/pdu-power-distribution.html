Power distribution units (PDU)

Distribution, protection and metering of electrical power, rack-mounted or on-board.

A PDU (Power Distribution Unit) distributes and protects power between a source and the equipment it feeds. The choice comes down to the current rating, the number and type of outlets, and above all the level of intelligence : from basic PDUs to managed PDUs with per-outlet metering and network monitoring.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • Current & powerRated current (A) and total permissible power.
  • OutletsNumber and type of outlets (C13/C19, industrial).
  • Voltage & phasesSingle-phase or three-phase.
  • Feature levelBasic, metered, switched, managed.
  • MonitoringSNMP, Modbus, web interface, per-outlet metering.
  • ProtectionFuses, circuit breakers, surge protection.
  • Form factorHorizontal 1U or vertical (0U) rack mount.
  • CertificationsCE and UL compliance depending on the market.
