Sensors & instrumentation

Current, voltage, temperature and pressure measurement for monitoring and control.

Sensors and instrumentation convert a physical quantity (current, voltage, temperature, pressure) into a usable signal. Accuracy, measurement range and output type (analog or digital) determine how well a sensor fits the measurement chain, from BMS to energy monitoring.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • Measured quantityCurrent, voltage, temperature, pressure.
  • Range / spanNominal measurement range.
  • AccuracyMeasurement error and linearity.
  • OutputAnalog (V/mA) or digital (I²C, SPI, bus).
  • BandwidthResponse speed.
  • IsolationGalvanic isolation for HV.
  • Power supplyVoltage and consumption.
  • CertificationsIP, ATEX depending on the environment.
