Cables & connectors

Power connectors, harnesses and high-voltage interconnection solutions.

Cabling and connectors provide reliable electrical interconnection, often at high voltage and in harsh environments. Current rating, ingress protection (IP) and safety features (HVIL interlock) are the key criteria in automotive and energy applications.

// WHAT TO COMPARE

Selection criteria

  • Rated current & voltageCurrent-carrying capacity and dielectric strength.
  • Contact countPower / signal configuration.
  • Ingress protectionIP67, IP69K for embedded use.
  • HV safetyHVIL interlock, touch protection.
  • Material & temperatureService range, thermal endurance.
  • LockingLatching system and keying.
  • StandardsUSCAR, LV214, IEC 61984.
  • Form factorStraight, right-angle, shielded.
